# The MET Museum — Hudson River School cover sources

All works below are marked public domain by The Metropolitan Museum of Art and are provided through [The Met Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources). The bundled files are normalized from the high-resolution `primaryImage` returned by The Met Collection API.

- `hudson-river-scene-kensett.jpg` — [Hudson River Scene](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/11310), John Frederick Kensett.
- `river-scene-durand.jpg` — [River Scene](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/10796), Asher Brown Durand.
- `landscape-lagoon-new-rochelle.jpg` — [Landscape and Lagoon, New Rochelle](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/11254), David Johnson.
- `on-hudson-near-idlewild.jpg` — [On the Hudson River, Near Idlewild](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/904641), Eliza Pratt Greatorex.
- `hackensack-meadows.jpg` — [Hackensack Meadows](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/14426), Jasper Francis Cropsey.
- `the-oxbow.jpg` — [View from Mount Holyoke, Northampton, Massachusetts, after a Thunderstorm—The Oxbow](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/10497), Thomas Cole.
- `heart-of-the-andes.jpg` — [Heart of the Andes](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/10481), Frederic Edwin Church.
- `the-parthenon.jpg` — [The Parthenon](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/10482), Frederic Edwin Church.
