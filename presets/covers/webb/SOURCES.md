These images are bundled as local NaitFlow cover presets for informational use. NASA imagery does not imply NASA endorsement. See the [NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).

- `webb-cosmic-cliffs-carina.jpg` — [Cosmic Cliffs in the Carina Nebula](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/webbs-first-images/). Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI.
- `webb-stephans-quintet.png` — [Stephan’s Quintet](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/stephans-quintet-nircam-and-miri-composite-image/). Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI.
- `webb-southern-ring-nebula.jpg` — [Notion `webb3.jpg` cover derivative](https://app.notion.com/images/page-cover/webb3.jpg) of the Southern Ring Nebula. Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI.
- `webb-first-deep-field.jpg` — [Webb’s First Deep Field](https://images.nasa.gov/details/webb_first_deep_field). Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI.
